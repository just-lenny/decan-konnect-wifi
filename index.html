Questions

 

Do my customers need an account?

No. They open your page, pay or enter a voucher, and get online.

Does Decan Konnect receive my customers' money?

No. Each business configures its own Till or Paybill. The platform never defaults payments to a central account.

Can a website alone block internet access?

No. A compatible router (MikroTik first) enforces access. The setup guide walks you through connecting it.

What happens if my router goes offline?

The portal pauses sales instead of taking money it cannot deliver on. Anyone who already paid keeps their purchase and is connected when the router returns.
