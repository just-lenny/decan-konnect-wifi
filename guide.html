Business setup guide

The same steps appear in the in-app setup wizard once the platform is live.

A captive portal website cannot restrict internet by itself. A compatible router must enforce access, and paid Wi-Fi should only be announced after you have tested it with a real phone.

1. Register and choose your address

Create your business and pick a short portal address, e.g. /decan-cyber. Names like admin, api, login and dashboard are reserved. Each address is unique.

2. Add your branding

Display name, logo link, welcome message, support phone and WhatsApp, terms and privacy text.

3. Create packages

Pick from 15/30/45/60 minutes, 1/5/8/12 hours, 1/7/14 days or 1 month. You set every price, and optional data and speed limits.

4. Set up your own M-Pesa

Pay-then-verify: customers pay your Till/Paybill and submit the SMS code; you confirm it from your statement before access starts. Instant prompt: needs your Safaricom Daraja app, your passkey and a shortcode enabled for STK Push, then a KES 1 test payment to your own phone. Never share your M-Pesa PIN.

5. Connect your MikroTik router

  • RouterOS v7 with working internet, correct clock and a HotSpot on your guest network.
  • Generate the setup script in the dashboard and paste it into Winbox's New Terminal.
  • Wait for the router to show Connected. It only does so after a real contact.
  • Keep router admin closed to the internet.

6. Test, then publish

  1. Join the Wi-Fi with a phone: it should open your portal.
  2. Redeem a voucher: internet works, and stops when time ends.
  3. Pay KES 1 or the cheapest package and confirm access.
  4. Publish in Preview, Voucher-only or Live mode.
